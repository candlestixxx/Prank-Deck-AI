# Aggressive Ideas for the Future

1. **Custom Sample Uploads:** Allow users to drag and drop local `.wav` or `.mp3` files into the Soundboard grid and persist them in `IndexedDB`.
2. **Audio Canvas Visualizer:** Add an HTML5 canvas to the Voice Studio to visualize the waveform of the recorded audio or the real-time synth sounds.
3. **Rust/WASM DSP:** Offload the pitch-shifting and monster effects to a WebAssembly module compiled from Rust for higher performance and crazy new DSP chains.
